# googlePKM

A personal calendar-automation application for a single user.

googlePKM connects to Google Calendar so that a personal task-management
system can:

- Read your existing calendar events.
- Create calendar events you explicitly request.
- Produce a daily briefing that includes your schedule.

googlePKM is for personal use by its owner only. It is not distributed to
other users, and it does not access Gmail or Google Drive.
